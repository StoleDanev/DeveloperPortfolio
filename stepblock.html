StepBlock Overview

Technologies: Java, Android Hardware Sensors (Step Counter API), Foreground Services, App Monetization

StepBlock is an innovative Android application designed to help users combat phone addiction by turning physical motion into currency for screen time.

Download on Google Play Privacy Policy

Key Features & Implementation

  • Hardware Sensor Integration: Implemented precise logic leveraging system step-counter sensors to track user movement in real time.
  • Dynamic App Blocking: Developed background foreground services to intercept and block selected addictive apps until step milestones are fulfilled.
  • Monetization & Success: Successfully launched and monetized on the Google Play store, achieving over 1,000+ installs and generating more than $100 in revenue.
  • Privacy First: Built as a local-first application where activity data remains stored safely on the user's device. Review the full Privacy Policy.
